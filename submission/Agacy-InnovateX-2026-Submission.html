Agacy · Agentic Privacy01
The problem

Agent autonomy currently comes with two unsafe defaults.

01 / PUBLIC BY DEFAULT

Every payment becomes permanent intelligence.

Balances, payment amounts, supplier pricing, operating cadence, and treasury runway can be profiled once an agent wallet is linked to its owner.

02 / PROMPT AS POLICY

“Do not overspend” is guidance, not authority.

A prompt-injected or malfunctioning model can ignore a textual limit when the same agent still controls an unrestricted signing path.

Owners should be able to delegate an outcome without publishing their finances or surrendering root control.
Problem · Privacy and authority02
The solution

Agacy separates intelligence from authority.

AI AGENT

Plans the work

Turns a natural-language goal into observations, verified tools, decisions, clarification, and honest refusal.

POLICY LAYER

Controls what may happen

Payment details must match the owner’s goal, imported vendor profile, custody path, and spending authorization.

CONFIDENTIAL SETTLEMENT

Protects financial values

Token-2022 Confidential Transfer encrypts the payment amount and confidential token balances on Solana.

The model decides what to do. The policy decides what it is allowed to do.
